**Figure 15.** *(provisional caption — figure and wording still to be tweaked.)* **The tiled scaled-RF model — one point-set, drawn as it runs.** The per-point-set circuit is identical to the two-point-set model; the four differences from it are marked **◆1–◆4**. Bar profiles are illustrative; counts, extents and constants are real.

**(A) The stimulus.** Two counter-rotating dot fields at 81°/s. One point-set is circled — point-set **1** of the bias-map figure above, at polar angle 0° — and it is drawn as its **V1 RF**, at 1σ solid with 2σ and 3σ dotted, the same way the RF is drawn in B.

**(B) One point-set.** The V1 RF feeds a motion hypercolumn and a colour hypercolumn. Each drive is multiplied by the cooperative gain (×), divided by its own normalizer (÷), and the resulting responses feed the pool **E**. The attentional bias enters at the **pool afferent** — on motion only; colour is unweighted — which is why it is drawn at that operator rather than at the front. The bias shown is `1+a_θ` at point-set 1, which prefers **88°**; it is the bias-map figure's top row with 1 added. **◆1** a different location gets a different peak, whereas the two-point-set model used one fixed field at 90° for both cells. The pool returns **one scalar gain to both hypercolumns**: gain = 1 + CoopL·E/(1+E/E_max), with CoopL 0.20, E_max 50 (**◆4**, bounded pool) and τ_E 20 ms. **E sums over channels, never over point-sets**, so the cooperative pool has no spatial extent of its own — its footprint *is* this RF.

**(C) The normalizer (◆2).** A Gaussian neighbourhood average over an effective 124–254 surrounding point-sets, none of them otherwise in this figure. Motion and colour are normalized **separately** (featureNorm), which leaves the cooperative pool as the only route between them. The normalizer's 1σ is 3.025 × the RF's — ⚠️ **never fitted**, an inherited pixel literal. The two-point-set model used one denominator over both cells and both features.

**(D) The read-out stage (◆3).** MT — translation, 8 units, position-**independent**; this is what the 8-AFC judgement reads. MST — rotation, CW/CCW, a position-**dependent** template. V4 — colour, 8 hue units. All by rectified-cosine pooling over point-sets and channels. The two-point-set model reads V1 channels directly and has no read-out stage at all.

**◆1** position-dependent bias map · **◆2** spatial, feature-separated normalizer · **◆3** MT/MST/V4 read-out stage · **◆4** bounded pool. The pool time constant, formerly a fifth difference, has matched the two-point-set model since 2026-08-24.
