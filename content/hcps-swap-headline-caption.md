**Figure 17.** *(provisional caption — figure and wording still to be tweaked.)* **The tiled point-set model on the real rotating stimulus: cueing is present in all four swap conditions.** 24 layouts per cell, cue lead 750 ms — the experiment's own — unless marked, at the τ_E 20 ms operating point. **Direction is the only thing cued.** Solid bars are the **cued** arm, pale bars the **uncued** arm; condition is given by position and label.

**(A, B) The neuronal measure** for the two geometries — S&B, 1473 point-sets on 19 rings; Çatak, 1077 on 14. All four conditions are positive: N +0.1401, M +0.1290 (S&B); N +0.1248, M +0.1173 (Çatak). Open diamonds ◇ are the same cells at a 300 ms cue lead. The dashed line in B is the human Çatak d′ ratio of 1.21; the human S&B value of 2.9 is far above this axis.

**C ≡ N and MC ≡ M exactly** — with no colour bias, a colour swap cannot reach the motion units. And **N and M are not distinguishable**: the spread is 0.007–0.011 against SEs of 0.006–0.014, and the direction flipped at the 2026-08-24 operating-point change.

**(C, D) The same cells with Poisson read-out noise**, gain fixed, 24 × 200 trials. The cueing benefit is +53.1 pp (no swap) and +29.4 pp (motion swap) for S&B; +47.1 and +22.2 pp for Çatak. Chance is 12.5%.

⛔ **What this figure does and does not claim.** A positive index is consistent with object-based **persistence**, but equally with the direction cue simply **re-acquiring** the other surface after the swap. This design cannot separate the two, so the claim is "present in all four conditions", **not** "survives the swaps".

⚠️ The behavioural benefit runs about **3× the human values** (S&B replication +16.8 pp, MCvsDb +14.6 pp), and the swap reduction (−33% / −37% on d′) exceeds MCvsDb's −21%. In both geometries that reduction comes **entirely from the uncued arm improving**, not from the cued arm falling — which remains an open question about the model.
