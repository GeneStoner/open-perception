**Figure 10.** *(draft caption — figure still to be tweaked.)* **Model IV — the bias acts on the POOL.** The same circuit, with the attentional bias moved. Nothing tuned reaches the gain at all: the gain is the single scalar `C`, which is why it arrives as a bare line and gets no panel of its own. The bias instead multiplies the **response** on its way to the pool, and the pool sums that product. One consequence is visible in the figure and one is not: the whole column is modulated by a single shared scalar, so it moves nearly uniformly — where Model III modulates it channel by channel.
